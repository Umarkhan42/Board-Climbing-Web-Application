FROM REDDIT


Quick log ascent that make every climb the grade that the setter put. Very convenient as a climber but it’s one of the reason that make grades all over the place


I see 2 problems with kilters system.

Is the problem a user previously stated - quick logs confirm the grade. You can easily see this with very obvious soft climbs but there's hardly any deviation from the assigned grade. If you look at moon or tension, the community submitted grades are generally more wide in deviation because you need put in extra effort to submit a grade.

Community submitted grades don't change the assigned grade. I think a system similar to stokt works better for the kilter since it doesn't have a moderation team like moon. The downside is that boulders tend to get sandbagged, but kilter is already super soft so it evens out.


The UI is messy and could use an overhaul. Also the app is relatively laggy and slow at times.

It really needs benchmarks. And like others said, disconnect the quick log from a vote on the grade.

Why do I need to pick my gym to even connect to the board or see climbs? Seems pretty odd unless there's some Bluetooth identification going on.

Dear God please debounce searching like what the fuck. Why does every character press while I'm typing trigger a search and lag my phone while searching 10k climbs in memory. Please debounce it so that it searches when I'm done typing

Benchmarks.

Ability to search and filter my own logbooks so I can see which climbs I've done of a certain grade and when.


Okay here is a massive one: the boards for kilter have many layouts (full ride, mainline, 7x10, 10x10, 12x12, homewall vs gym).

It would be REALLY cool if you could sort by popularity for specific layouts. For example. Let’s say you have all the holds on your gym/home wall. If you sort by popularity it will often show the most popular routes being the smaller holds packs (because it’s cheaper so more people/gyms purchase). So you get a route that does not utilize all the holds you have, but instead a lesser setup.

When you pay more money for more holds, you want the routes to use them all. Would be really cool to sort for routes using the full extent of holds pack that you/your gym have.


When you look at the graph of all of your sends you cant click on the bar chart to drill into specifics grades.
The list of climbs is sorted only by date so if you want to find something specific you need to scroll until you find what you want by date descending. Would be helping to be able to sort the breakdown in the graph or list by other methods.


Tabs could probably be condensed to notifications, boards and profile. Kilter tab is pretty irrelevant unless they incentivize uploading videos and there is an opportunity for your climb to be on that tab. Online kilter competition or announcements

Make it so you can find your liked climbs directly instead of adding filter.

Search climbs of people you follow with a list filled of those you follow

When sorting by least or most repeated, be able to set more or less than x ascents.

Obviously fix grading system, literally everyone knows its bad.
